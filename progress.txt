- **Diagnosis**: 
  1. Vite default build only bundled `index.html`, omitting `settings.html` from `dist/`. When Tauri attempted to open the dedicated settings window, it was missing from the packaged distribution.
  2. Tab switching logic collapsed drawers into small square dimensions (`340x74`), truncating Chat and Settings contents.

  3. Sized drawers dynamically in `app/src/main.ts` (Chat: 400x340, Dictate: 400x300), and wired Settings to open the dedicated settings window via IPC command `open_settings_window`.
- **Result**: `npm run build` generates both `dist/index.html` (17.12 kB) and `dist/settings.html` (32.68 kB).
